Where do the world's new electric cars get sold?

China took 63 percent of the 21.2 million electric cars sold worldwide in 2025.

Thousands of new electric cars sold worldwide

  1. China sold 13.3 million, which is 63 percent of the world total.
  2. The United States ranks second at 1.53 million, roughly 8.7 times below China.
  3. The top five markets together hold 79 percent of world sales.
  4. World sales reached 21.2 million in 2025, 7.1 times the 2020 figure of 2.98 million.

Scale
Linear, not logarithmic. China dwarfs the rest, and a log scale would make that gap look smaller than it is.
Precision
The source publishes some markets rounded and some exact. Vietnam carries 180,077 against Belgium at 180,000, and both read 180 thousand here.

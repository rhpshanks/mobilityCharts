Which countries buy electric, measured as a share of new car sales?

Norway reached 97 percent in 2025. Eight countries now clear half of all new car sales.

Electric share of new car sales, 2025

Top 15 countries of the 59 carrying a 2025 value

The world figure, 2010 to 2025

Electric share of new car sales worldwide, percent

  1. Norway leads at 97 percent, ahead of Denmark at 71 percent.
  2. Eight countries sit at or above half: Norway, Denmark, Nepal, Iceland, Sweden, the Netherlands, Finland and China.
  3. Nepal ranks third at 68 percent, above Sweden and the Netherlands.
  4. Worldwide the share hit 25 percent in 2025, against 4.4 percent in 2020.
  5. 19 of the 59 countries with a 2025 value stand at or above 30 percent.
